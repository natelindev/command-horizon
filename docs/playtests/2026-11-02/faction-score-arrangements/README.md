[Independent decoded verification](verification.json) and [its log](encoded-verification.log) pass all source hashes, finite/unclipped audio, three equal-length stereo stems, required nonempty contributions, and reconstructed final auditions. Combined decoded peaks are −8.577/−3.900/−8.146 dBFS. Loop boundary steps are at most 0.000068. Reconstruction SNR against the lossless arranged audition is 27.870/28.390/21.920 dB after separate Vorbis encoding. Helix’s sub part has 99.537% of its measured energy between 25 and 90 Hz. Independent pitch tracking of four actual isolated guitar notes measures a continuous rise of about 1.99 semitones between early and settled windows, with settled pitches within 0.10 semitone of the intended note. The source trajectory begins five semitones below that target; this confirms an actual pitch slide rather than a static-note label.

To rebuild, install `tools/audio/music-stems-requirements.txt`, run `art/audio/fetch_faction_instruments.py fetch`, then `tools/audio/build_music_stems.py`. The builder accepts `--output` and `--evidence` to stage assets before publication. `art/audio/verify_faction_arrangements.py` audits those staged assets. Re-analysis with `art/audio/analyze_faction_score.py` is required if any full source recording changes. `--base-only` reconstructs the earlier source-only stems for comparison.

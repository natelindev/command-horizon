# Effect and scene lifetime verification — 2026-11-02

The original OpenGL collapse preview produced six texture-cleanup errors (two 349,524-byte allocations per Match) and an unsupported manual-GPU-particle warning. The current [native OpenGL preview](StructureCollapsePreview-opengl.log) passes with zero errors across three factions, actual dust/debris, all collapse stages, ruins and cannon effects. Its remaining BPTC-to-RGBA8 notices are hardware format conversion, unrelated to cleanup. [Actual falling-stage capture](../../../../art/structure-collapse/opengl/bastion-falling.png) was visually reviewed.

Two changes resolve the failures:

- Match retains its originating PackedScene after `NOTIFICATION_SCENE_INSTANTIATED`. The isolated original trigger, `load(path).instantiate()` followed by freeing the instance, leaked textures when the temporary PackedScene died first. Keeping the template through the instance lifetime avoids the failure without a global cache. [Six repeated Match/Skirmish instances](SceneResourceLifetimeRegression-opengl.log) now free cleanly; [actual native save/reload](SaveRestoreRegression-native.log) also passes. Original serialized viewport-texture bindings remain in place; the unsuccessful runtime binding/teardown experiment was removed.
- Compatibility uses a bounded MultiMesh particle buffer because that renderer cannot manually emit GPUParticles. Emission counts/capacities stay intact, and vertex shaders advance age, trajectory, growth and fade. CPU work scales with new emissions rather than every live particle. [OpenGL](ManualParticlesRegression-opengl.log) and [headless](ManualParticlesRegression-headless.log) checks cover ring replacement, velocity/ground/full-precision birth metadata, long-match clocks, pause and expiry.

Normal GPU particle buffers, debris, dust and the cannon muzzle ring are now included in production CombatPrewarm under the loading cover. [Rendered prewarm regression](CombatPrewarmRegression.log) verifies exact simulation/RNG/live-pool preservation, paused loading and cancellation. The performance fixture must call this actual production preparation after constructing its full roster; a cold-effects diagnostic remains distinct from the production benchmark. No timing samples are subtracted. [Combat presentation checks](CombatPresentationRegression.log) pass after caching the flat-map ground lookup and avoiding ground sampling for particle processors that do not consume it; counts remain unchanged.

The separate lava ember fix replaces an automatically generated ParticleProcessMaterial shader with an authored equivalent, preserving 96 emission points, four-second lifetime, upward drift and gradient. Its headless native/fallback and actual renderer evidence is retained in [the macro evidence folder](../macro-economy/EnvironmentEffectsRegression-native.log) and the parent integrated baseline. Strict 200-unit performance acceptance remains tracked separately; these fixes do not close that gate.

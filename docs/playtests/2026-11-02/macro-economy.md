# Macro economy benchmark verification

The new harvesting loop differentiates the three factions, supports forward drop-offs, and connects neutral economic objectives to active power, fuel and macro abilities. This report records the current mechanics, native runtime checks and measured home-field pacing.

## Fortification and maintenance

Bastion structures retain their separate +25% hull advantage and now reduce physical gun/rocket damage by 15%. Fractional damage remains exact, energy weapons ignore this protection, and frozen rounds retain the correct classification after their shooter dies. Bastion Repair Depots automatically maintain adjacent armor within 5 m, sharing the existing repair channel limits; mobile support continues to repair within 3 m. Neither service consumes resources. The [native](macro-economy/BastionFortificationRegression-native.log) and [script fallback](macro-economy/BastionFortificationRegression-fallback.log) regressions cover physical/energy distinctions, actual repairs, outside-range rejection, pause and full save restoration. The existing [combat suite](macro-economy/CombatRegression.log) remains clean.

Routine order acknowledgements now check the shared chatter cooldown before resolving the voice bank. The [audio regression](macro-economy/AudioEnhancementRegression.log) proves a 200-reply command burst performs one recording lookup while retaining accepted variants, ready announcements, priorities and captions.

## Home-field pacing

The [live cohort test](../../../tests/headless/HomeFieldPacingRegression.gd) uses two harvesters per material node and two per electronics node, real navigation/loading/delivery, no production, upgrades, refinery, strategic AI, resource grants or macro powers. It records actual node stocks, 60% expansion timing, depletion time, phase deliveries and the longest interval without material gathering. This distinguishes ordinary end-of-field throughput from a hung phase/return action.

The [original native baseline](../../../art/economy/home-field-pacing-baseline.json) found that five 400-material home piles exhausted in 276.1 / 352.0 / 335.0 seconds for Bastion / Helix / Frontier. This fails the intended 7–9 minute window. The final [30-cohort matrix](../../../art/economy/home-field-pacing.json) passes on three authored biomes and all seven legacy maps: 425.2–530.7 seconds, with no material-gathering stall longer than 3.25 seconds. Every cohort uses five 600-material home piles and two 720-electronics piles. Mule/Skimmer travel factors are explicit profile values (0.9 / 1.15), and Frontier retains its original travel factor. Fresh legacy skirmishes now retain the original resource node order and identities while placing home materials on safe roughly nine-metre HQ arcs. Tight advanced bases split that arc around their foundations; electronics can bend around its ends. All 308 deposits across 44 Standard/Advanced homes pass [native route, approach and starting-structure clearance checks](macro-economy/LegacyHomeNavigationRegression.log). The [seven-map save regression](macro-economy/LegacyHomeFieldsSaveRegression.log) verifies exact resource IDs, amounts, positions and field origins across restoration, and confirms campaign/restoring matches bypass new-match reconciliation. Existing saved deposit amounts, positions and IDs are preserved.

The original coastal Helix cohort exposed a landing bug: the inner-ring-first phase search selected the far side of the HQ, producing 565.4-second depletion. Actual channel/collection durations were correct. Searching clear radial rings along the field-facing lane first reduced the same cohort to 454.0 seconds. The [diagnostic](macro-economy/home-field-phase-diagnostic.log) retains worker-state totals and actual landing distances; [all ten repeated Helix cohorts](macro-economy/home-field-pacing-helix-final.log) pass. Bastion/Frontier profiles were unchanged by this fix, so the final matrix retains their completed measurements from the [authored](macro-economy/home-field-pacing-authored-before-landing-fix.log) and [legacy](macro-economy/home-field-pacing-legacy-before-landing-fix.log) runs.

## Related integration checks

The [native combat-audio regression](macro-economy/CombatAudioRegression.log) covers actual shield and hull damage, delayed weapon hits and exactly one impact cue for fully absorbed, overflowing and unshielded hits. The [voice regression](macro-economy/AudioEnhancementRegression.log) verifies the 200-member order burst resolves one voice-bank lookup, every own-faction order variant is loaded before input, and accepted replies retain their cast, captions, priority and playback.

The lava ember processor now uses an explicit particle shader with the existing 96-point budget, four-second lifetime, upward drift and color ramp. The [native environment regression](macro-economy/EnvironmentEffectsRegression-native.log), [script fallback](macro-economy/EnvironmentEffectsRegression-fallback.log) and [isolated teardown](macro-economy/EmberLifetimeProbe.log) all exit without RID cleanup warnings. The [Metal preview](macro-economy/EnvironmentEffectsPreview.log) passes and its [lava captures](../../../art/environment-effects/lava-high.png) retain visible ember flecks. Its frame measurements were recorded while synthesis workers were active and are diagnostic only.

## Limits

These controlled gameplay checks and optimal harvesting cohorts do not establish repeated human-match balance or 200-unit performance. Authored biome coverage uses three representative maps; legacy navigation coverage includes every start and both Standard/Advanced bases. New combat barks and campaign narration remain tracked separately.

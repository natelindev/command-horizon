# Desert Frontier environment — 2026-10-03

The desert now has layered sandstone, an abandoned extraction/freight identity, varied sand and haul-road materials, low ruins, sage scrub and a surrounding canyon landscape. This pass develops the existing playable layout and its original desert concept. It does not close final art, full-match balance or broader release acceptance.

## Implemented composition

Ten original Blender assets are generated by [`build_desert.py`](../../../art/environment/build_desert.py), with editable collections in [`DesertKit.blend`](../../../art/environment/DesertKit.blend), evaluated bounds in the [manifest](../../../art/environment/desert-manifest.json), and imported GLBs under `assets/models/environment/desert`.

| Asset family | Count | Use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| SandstoneShelf A–D | 4 | Four existing interior blockers, with eroded strata, weighhouse remnants and utility hardware |
| CanyonMesa 1–3 | 3 | Eleven varied surrounding mesas, all beyond playable space |
| FrontierExtractionRig / AbandonedFreightBay | 2 | Tower and two freight shelters outside the boundary |
| DryScrub | 1 | Fourteen exterior and twelve small interior sage clusters |

Interior models remain within the original 5.8 m radius / 1.15 m height limits. Their measured maximums are 5.4805 m / 1.0954 m. Low walkable vegetation is below 0.3 m. All 28 exterior instances and their actual imported vertices lie outside the 128×96 playable rectangle; they have no gameplay collision, navigation or sight components. Their terrain has shaped elevations and flat landmark foundations, and meets the playable plane at matching height and upward normals.

World-space materials provide sand/iron patches, distance-filtered ripples, gravel shoulders, worn tracks, field branches, concrete joints and broken apron paint. Stone surfaces add weathering while retaining distinct strata and industrial materials. Warm sun and cooler ambient fill preserve unit contrast. Mesa caps were revised after review to remove radial triangle shading; the boundary terrain's height/normal transition was revised to remove a visible rectangular lighting seam.

The map owns separate environment and fog-material instances. Public exterior scenery fades in over the first six metres outside the map and fades away at the distant perimeter. Interior fog and enemy observation rules retain their existing behavior. Both factions' rendered hidden-base checks remain black, and a subsequent Open Plains match retains zero exterior visibility.

## Navigation correction found during verification

Repeated advance/archive/retreat runs exposed variable delays around the northern outer field. Diagnostics showed active tanks pushed away from their paths and steering back toward earlier intermediate waypoints; a baseline trace took 54 seconds to return all units, missing the existing 38.3-second retreat check. Increasing intermediate tolerance alone did not reliably solve it. Earlier replanning alone passed desert repetitions but left one of twelve units short in the shared crowd test.

The final basic-tank settings combine a 0.5 m intermediate waypoint tolerance with a 2 m maximum path deviation before replanning. Final destination tolerance remains 0.2 m, with the existing speed, radius, collision/avoidance and navigation constraints. Four independent 48-tank advance/archive/retreat runs, alternating factions, pass the original deadlines. Shared obstacle routing, 12/12 crowded arrivals, harvesting, factory egress, construction, dynamic obstacles and Alpine passes also pass. The engine documents the distinct [intermediate waypoint and path deviation controls](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_navigationagent3d.html#class-navigationagent3d-property-path-desired-distance); the values here are based on these game scenarios.

`FrontierCrowdRegression.gd` retains the repeated regression. Failure diagnostics include active action, destination, path and path index. The test has not gained a longer timeout or looser arrival boundary.

## Verification

- Original field positions, budgets (3,000 materials / 1,280 electronics), four shelf envelopes, three routes and archive identity are retained. The tested base route remains 36.7 seconds at 2.6 m/s; crossing the shelf costs 38.1 m on ground versus 34 m in air.
- Both factions exercise all three 16-tank approaches and retreats, a real disk snapshot/restoration while moving, shelf rejection and central-apron acceptance for construction, both starting economies and outer electronics return.
- Imported geometry is measured in Godot. Actual drones and strike aircraft cross both pairs of shelves. Ground-level rays are blocked; elevated rays and the open flank remain clear. Geometry and sight checks are repeated after restoration.
- Normal-clock Metal captures exercise ordinary home fog, hidden enemy bases, visible 14-unit combat, tactical pause, full overview, shelves, freight apron, boundary and extraction details at 1280×720, plus an actual 1440×900 wide view. The last rendered run emitted 75 shots and 70 impacts for each faction; simulation and shot counts freeze in tactical pause.
- Shared combat checks cover current observation, occlusion, legal firing, hidden-target loss, armor, repair and pause. Format, lint, editor import and whitespace checks are part of the final verification.

Reproduce with Godot 4.6:

```sh
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/DesertEnvironmentRegression.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/FrontierCrowdRegression.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/NavigationRegression.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/AlpineRegression.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/CombatRegression.gd
godot --path . --script tests/rendered/DesertEnvironmentPreview.gd
```

The first geometry harness had exact float comparisons and assumed that the headless renderer returned shader defaults; those fixture errors were corrected. The extraction-detail capture required preserving the camera's typed bounds array. These failures are separate from the reproduced movement and terrain-seam issues above.

## Remaining acceptance

This is a verified desert implementation pass. Broader environmental composition across the remaining maps, regional life variety, environmental audio, subjective final art review, direct physical-device input and repeated complete-match/novice balance remain open. Performance optimization remains deferred by user direction; these tests are not frame-rate acceptance. The headless engine still reports the existing navigation GPU-readback and shutdown ObjectDB/shader-resource warnings. The rendered pass has no script errors.

# Alpine Relay environment — 2026-10-03

Alpine Relay now has fractured glacial shelves, snowy mountain surroundings, conifers and abandoned communications landmarks. Connected plowed routes and weathered station aprons replace isolated paving blocks; snow, ice and gravel give the terrain variation. This advances the established alpine setting while preserving the two passes, rear bypass, paired economy and infantry-captured vision objectives.

## Editable environment kit

Ten original models are generated by [`build_alpine.py`](../../../art/environment/build_alpine.py), with independent editable collections in [`AlpineKit.blend`](../../../art/environment/AlpineKit.blend), evaluated bounds in the [manifest](../../../art/environment/alpine-manifest.json), and GLBs under `assets/models/environment/alpine`. No external models or textures are required.

Fractured granite, broken survey footings and exposed conduit fit inside the existing shelf envelopes. Mountain crests were sharpened after the first rendered review; separate patches of branch snow expose the green conifers. Cool ambient light and restrained warm sunlight retain army contrast. Stone and snow weathering applies only to the matching imported materials, preserving metal, glazing and station paint.

All 28 exterior instances, including their actual imported vertices, lie outside the 128×104 playable rectangle. Surrounding terrain has rolling elevations and flattened foundations; its inner edge meets the playable plane at zero height with matching upward normals. Exterior geometry contributes no collision, navigation, sight or selectable entities. Low sedge stays below 0.3 m.

The map owns its lighting and fog material instances. Distant public scenery fades in outside the map boundary. Interior unexplored ground and enemy observation retain their existing rules; rendered hidden-base pixels remain black for both factions, and a subsequent Open Plains match retains zero exterior visibility.

## Objective readability

The shared relay label now uses larger text and a stronger dark outline, which remains readable over snow. Owner-colored lamps and explicit capture percentage / SECURED / CONTESTED / HOSTILE text provide complementary state cues. Capture time, radius, vision, ownership and archive data are unchanged.

The rendered review uses real registered infantry to capture and recapture the northern relay and an opposing tank to contest it. Their weapons are disabled only in this isolated fixture. An observing drone outside capture range keeps the hostile relay legitimately visible. Capture progress freezes in tactical pause. Separate headless checks retain partial-recapture disk restoration and AI objective assignment.

Reproduce with Godot 4.6:

```sh
blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/environment/build_alpine.py
godot --headless --editor --path . --import
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/AlpineEnvironmentRegression.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script tests/headless/CombatRegression.gd
godot --path . --script tests/rendered/AlpineEnvironmentPreview.gd
```

The initial geometry fixture reused a desert flank ray that crossed the next legitimate alpine shelf. It now tests the actual open passes. The combined environment test has an explicit simulation deadline covering twelve aircraft crossings, three tank routes, two archives and both economies per faction.

# Command Horizon — 2026-10-03

Operation 06 is available from **Campaign → Horizon**. Capture a forward deployment zone with infantry, secure it for 30 cumulative uncontested seconds, then destroy Helix command while keeping infantry at deployment. The final operation combines a defended position, a separate strike force, repair coverage, aircraft, paid replacements and reuse of saved programs.

## Delivered loop

The 184×132 map has a western staging base, central deployment yard, eastern command compound and a continuous high berm. Ground forces normally use the southern pass. Broken Foundry completion opens an actual northern ground passage on fresh starts; the saved route determines geometry on reload, independently of subsequent campaign progress. Aircraft cross the berm directly. The terrain, roads and repeated cliff shapes remain gameplay blockouts. The user's requested later environment pass will add landmarks, terrain/material variety, industrial detail, atmosphere and environmental storytelling while retaining playable routes and readable combat.

The normal Bastion start includes an HQ, three generators, barracks, vehicle factory, command lab, repair depot and aircraft factory. The mobile force has four engineers, twelve infantry, eight chipped tanks, four combat walkers, two railgun tanks, two repair robots, two strike aircraft and a scout. Funds start at 180 materials and 100 electronics. Basic programs and Coordination are available; assignments remain explicit player choices. The normal harvesting, building, production and authoring systems remain usable throughout the mission.

Infantry capture the mast in ten seconds within three metres. Enemy ground combatants contest. Friendly infantry must remain for 30 cumulative uncontested seconds; an empty owned zone does not advance this timer. Leaving, contest and recapture retain secured time. After deployment is secured, victory requires both enemy HQ destruction and current uncontested deployment ownership with friendly infantry present. Destroying the HQ early does not bypass deployment, and losing deployment does not respawn the HQ. Losing the player's HQ starts the usual 60-second rebuild window.

The forward defense has 4/6/8 infantry on Story/Standard/Veteran, two skimmers and a Mender. The southern entrance has four infantry, two skimmers and two ground turrets along its approach. The HQ has 6/8/10 infantry, two Lancers, two Prism artillery units, two Wraith aircraft, two Menders and a nearby repair depot; a northern AA turret contests the air approach. Enemy assignments use ordinary sight, weapons, preparation, bounded Guard/Fire support and service rules. Factories are scenery/targets in this operation and do not produce replacement defenders.

First friendly capture starts four finite response times, at 20, 50, 80 and 110 seconds after capture. Each is announced ten seconds ahead. A southeastern ground wing has 2/3/4 units (infantry plus one skimmer); a northeastern air wing has 1/1/2 Wraiths. A live friendly unit within twelve metres of an entry denies that wing permanently. There is no ongoing enemy income or unlimited production.

## Campaign, replay and archives

All six operations are directly selectable. The 720p selector uses short labels with full-title tooltips; objective locators and separate infantry, armor, air-wing and repair selection fit beside the shared controls. The deployment panel distinguishes enemy capture from friendly capture and stays above the minimap during the two-line deployment notice.

Finale victory unlocks two optional replay modifiers. **Lean deployment** starts with two engineers, eight infantry, four tanks and 80 materials / 40 electronics; all other required unit roles and programming capabilities remain. **Reinforced garrison** adds four HQ infantry and one ground unit to each response. Difficulty is independent of the modifier. Progress schema 6 reads schemas 1–5 and preserves their completion/reward flags. Finale completion and each challenge are recorded separately. The campaign-complete result requires all six operations, rather than treating a direct finale win as completion of earlier missions.

Gameplay snapshots remain version 5. The mission record preserves deployment phase and secured time, ownership/candidate/capture/contest, enemy HQ identity or destruction, HQ recovery, response clock/warnings/counts, chosen challenge and alternate-route geometry. Restored games remain paused. Invalid references, unbounded response counts, inconsistent phases, unknown challenges and already-completed states are rejected. Start, friendly capture, secured deployment and HQ destruction request rotating checkpoints.

## Verification

- `CommandHorizonRules.gd` exercises actual capture/contest/empty-zone suspension, deployment completion, HQ destruction without infantry, return-to-zone victory, HQ defeat, all nine difficulty/modifier budgets, response caps and occupied-entry denial. Actual ground traversal and navigation paths prove that the Foundry reward opens a shorter northern route. Real file round trips cover partial capture, secured time, destroyed HQ, both route geometries and both modifiers; paused mission state is unchanged. Progress schemas 1–5 migrate without losing badges and challenge completions accumulate correctly.
- The archive scenarios exposed turrets discarding restored actions because their ordinary order guard rejected every replacement. Both turret types now accept the internal restoration action while still rejecting ordinary movement orders. The regression compares exact archived actions/timers and verifies actual ground and air damage after restoration. Shared shooting/cooldown, pause and repeat-damage restoration checks also pass.
- `CommandHorizonRegression.gd` uses ordinary orders, saved Guard/Service versions, strike-and-return aircraft, harvesting and paid infantry/repair/tank replacements. The verified Standard south-route run secures deployment at **51.23 simulated seconds** and wins at **177.60 seconds**. The Foundry-supported north-route run wins at **153.80 seconds**. Both use all four responses and fire aircraft weapons. Neither edits enemy HP, removes enemies, adds funds or forces objective state in those playthroughs.
- `CommandHorizonPlaythrough.gd` runs the same south-route commander with native Metal rendering at 1280×720 and normal simulation time. It wins at **163.62 simulated seconds**, with 29 mobile units surviving. Result: **zero failures**. This is a playable-route result, not a frame-rate measurement or novice pacing acceptance.
- Both optional modifiers also have actual Standard-difficulty south-route victories. **Reinforced** wins at **223.43 seconds**. **Lean** wins at **293.78 seconds** after buying two engineers, assigning both resource types, earning the funds for four tank jobs, holding through the finite responses and redirecting air/scout support toward the command compound. Its paid support and infantry replacements use ordinary queues. Both final runs report **zero failures** without runtime script errors. These demonstrate viable strategies; they do not establish Story/Veteran or novice balance.
- `CommandHorizonPreview.gd` verifies all six briefings, selection shortcuts, objective/minimap separation with a two-line notice, keyboard start, tactical pause, automatic checkpoint, manual save/dialog/load, Resume, result, replay and Campaign return. Its controlled result fixture is distinct from the real combat commander. The final layout run reports **zero failures**. `CommandHorizonChallengePreview.gd` was exercised with native desktop mouse/Down/Return input; the selected Lean variant launched with four tanks and reports **zero failures**. Direct synthetic popup keys were unreliable, so they are not claimed as evidence of native keyboard behavior.
- Shared First Orders and active-combat archive regressions pass. The six-operation selector also passes the existing Broken Foundry UI flow. Formatting/lint tools are now available locally; touched-code format/lint, Godot import and whitespace checks pass. Existing navigation-bake readback and shutdown shader/resource diagnostics remain.

Earlier commanders failed after infantry drifted from the mast or the guard retreated beyond its assigned repair coverage. The retained commander separates infantry positions from armor, covers the guard's actual retreat point with a saved service area, buys replacements and reuses the same doctrines for the strike force. Lean rush attempts held deployment but stalled against the HQ; redirecting aircraft alone was insufficient. Its successful strategy first rebuilds the economy and funds a larger assault. The first economic-recovery victory also exposed a premature purchase assertion; the final commander waits for actual earnings before buying each tank. These failures are retained as evidence, not converted into claims of general balance.

## Remaining acceptance

Standard routes take about three minutes for a practiced scripted commander; the tested modifiers take roughly four to five minutes. Human onboarding, novice pacing, full Story/Veteran combat balance and repeatability across modifiers remain acceptance work. The six mission loops are present, but final environments/art/audio, remaining faction/map scope and release/platform acceptance are still unfinished. Performance optimization stays deferred.

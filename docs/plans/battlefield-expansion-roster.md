# New skirmish battlefields

All maps use equal starting resources at 18 m from each HQ. Two-player maps have eight resource fields and six facilities; four-player maps have twelve of each. Layouts use rotational symmetry. Four-player games are free-for-all against three AI commanders.

| Map | Players | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Redrock Divide | 2 | Three canyon crossings with rich outer flanks. |
| Saltwind Basin | 2 | A pale salt pan: exposed center, sheltered edge expansions. |
| Pinebreak Valley | 2 | Wooded islands split a winding valley into ambush lanes. |
| Glacier Narrows | 2 | Frozen meltwater divides two broad mountain passes. |
| Emberworks | 2 | Abandoned furnaces and service alleys around a central works. |
| Cinder Ring | 4 | A black volcanic caldera with an open outer circuit. |
| Four Rivers | 4 | Four headwater pools separate spokes into a shared heartland. |
| Sunken Exchange | 4 | Flooded port basins leave a cross of cargo roads. |
| Frostbound Crown | 4 | A crown of glacial shelves guards the central repair network. |
| Copper Badlands | 4 | Staggered copper mesas create diagonal flanking corridors. |
| Verdant Commons | 4 | Sheltered groves ring a broad, resource-rich common. |
| Railspire Junction | 4 | Rail depots divide a grid of long industrial sightlines. |
| Shattered Oasis | 4 | A turquoise oasis forces clockwise expansion around its shores. |

# Campaign voice and portrait credits

The hero conversations are original Open RTS dialogue, synthesized offline with
Chatterbox TTS by Resemble AI (MIT). English recordings carry the model's Perth
watermark. Chinese text is provided as captions; all playback is local.

- Chatterbox: https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox
- Model: https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox
- CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92: Christophe Veaux, Junichi Yamagishi and Kirsten MacDonald
  (2019), University of Edinburgh, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645,
  CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Cleaned VCTK references and volunteer CC0 voice donations: Kyutai's Unmute
  Voice Donation Project, https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices and
  https://unmute.sh/voice-donation

Thirteen distinct reference recordings are assigned in
`art/audio/campaign-cast.json`. Aegis, Nyx and Rook retain their battle voices;
Voss, Kerrigan, Elias, CANTOR, Arden, Ren, Ilan, Wren, Hask and Jory each use a
separate reference. References are build inputs and are not shipped.

Processing includes silence trimming, speech equalization, light faction radio
coloring, loudness normalization, peak limiting and 24 kHz Ogg encoding. CANTOR
also uses a fixed-pitch vocoder and resonant processing. Each generated file's
source hash, reference identity check and transcription result are retained in
`art/audio/campaign-audio-manifest.json`. The separate shipped-file audit measures
intelligibility, loudness and cast distinctness after decoding the Oggs.

The SVG portraits are original geometric illustrations generated by
`tools/ui/generate_campaign_portraits.py`, distributed under the repository's
MIT license. They depict fictional characters and do not reproduce voice donors.

Legacy single-narrator recordings remain available for older saves; their
production records are in `assets/audio/campaign/generation-manifest.json`. They use Kokoro-82M and
Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh (Apache 2.0), with English `af_heart` and Chinese `zf_001`
voices: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M and
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh.

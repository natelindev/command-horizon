Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless grayscale albedo texture for armored vehicles and industrial sci-fi buildings in a top-down real-time strategy game.
Primary request: create one square, edge-to-edge tileable texture of lightly worn painted armor panels, intended to be multiplied by an existing faction paint color in a game shader.
Composition/framing: perfectly flat orthographic straight-on surface scan; the entire image is the material, no object, no border, no perspective. Broad rectangular interlocking service panels, sparse narrow panel joints, occasional tiny recessed fasteners, subtle chipped paint and grime concentrated at joints. Panel widths varied but deliberately simple and readable from an RTS camera. Match opposite edges seamlessly in both axes.
Color palette: strictly neutral achromatic grayscale, predominantly very light gray/off-white, restrained medium-gray joints, very few dark marks. No colored pixels or faction colors.
Lighting: pure diffuse base color, even unlit illumination; no directional shadows, no highlights, no reflections, no ambient shading gradients.
Style: restrained stylized realism, clean manufactured surfaces with believable selective wear.
Constraints: one texture only, no labels, no text, no logos, no emblems, no warning stripes, no glow, no large rust patches, no deep 3D recesses, no vignette. A flat color map, not a shaded material preview or a grid of multiple maps.

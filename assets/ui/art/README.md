# River city menu artwork

`river-city-menu.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into this project. It is a background illustration; all text and interactive controls are native Godot UI.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed widescreen strategy game main-menu background, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: polished artistic key art for Open RTS, an industrial frontier strategy game. A living river city at golden hour: layered concrete and brick workshops, residential terraces, warm inhabited windows, bridges, quays, small civilian traffic, distant forested mountain ridges, a few heavy near-future armored vehicles on the right foreground. Grounded restrained science fiction, no fantasy.
Style/medium: richly painted cinematic environment concept art, sophisticated atmospheric perspective, crisp architectural focal details with painterly distance, premium PC strategy game.
Composition/framing: panoramic elevated view down a winding river through the city, strongest detail in the right two thirds. The left third has darker quiet foreground architecture and mist, usable behind live menu typography. Full image edge to edge.
Lighting/mood: amber sun breaking through storm clouds, cool slate shadows, soft water reflections, subtle city lights. Warm copper and muted teal accents.
Constraints: artwork only, no text, no logo, no UI buttons, no frames, no watermark.

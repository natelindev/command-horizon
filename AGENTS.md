# Agent instructions

## Battle HUD

- Battle HUD textures are generated by `tools/ui/generate_hud_kit.py` (one kit per faction under `assets/ui/hud/<faction>/`); change the generator profiles instead of editing the PNGs.
- Review visual HUD changes with `tests/rendered/HudConsolePreview.gd` (run without `--headless`), which writes captures for every faction to `art/hud-console/`.

## Ground and model textures

- Ground scans in `assets/textures/terrain/` come from `tools/textures/build_terrain_textures.py` and decals in `assets/textures/decals/` from `tools/textures/generate_ground_decals.py`; change the scripts instead of editing the images.
- Faction GLBs must be exported by their `art/<faction>/build_*.py` scripts, which bake vertex weathering through `art/common/weathering.py`; do not edit the GLBs directly.
- Review ground changes with `tests/rendered/TerrainLayersPreview.gd` (`art/terrain-layers/`) and model weathering with `tests/rendered/WeatheringPreview.gd` (`art/weathering/`), both without `--headless`; `tests/headless/RichTexturesRegression.gd` covers the contracts.
